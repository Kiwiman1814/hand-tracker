Hand Tracker

Tracks up to two hands with your phone camera and shows how much each finger is curled (0 % = straight, 100 % = fist). Pinch thumb and index finger together on a block to pick it up, then solve the puzzles by getting every block onto the square of its own colour. The 3D button shows your hand as a wire mesh next to a robot hand that copies it.

This is just a test, not a current project. Try the spell-casting version
